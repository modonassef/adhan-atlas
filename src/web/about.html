Privacy and credits

Adhan Atlas أطلس الأذان is a live world map of the five daily prayers, with prayer times, the Qibla and a mosque display for any place.

Your privacy

  • There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no cookies.
  • Your settings and saved places are kept in your own browser, on your device. Clearing the site's data removes them.
  • If you tap Use my location, your device tells the page where you are so it can work out your prayer times. That happens on your device; your position is not sent anywhere. The Qibla compass works the same way.
  • Everything the page needs, its code, map, town list and fonts, comes from this site, so no other company learns that you visited.
  • Like any website, the hosting service may keep ordinary request logs, such as IP addresses, for a short time.

About the times

Times are calculated on your device from the position of the sun, using the method you choose (or the one most used in each country). They usually agree with published timetables to within a minute or two, but mosques and local authorities may adjust them. Check with your local mosque for the timetable it follows.
